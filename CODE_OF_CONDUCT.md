# Code of conduct

Short version: argue with the work, not the person.

- **Corrections are welcome and expected.** Telling us a verdict is wrong, with evidence, is the
  highest-value contribution here. Nobody gets defensive about it.
- **Failure is not embarrassing.** This library exists because most people hide theirs. Do not mock
  anyone for what did not work, ours or yours.
- **Say what you measured.** Confident guesses dressed as measurements waste everyone's time and
  will be reverted.
- **Agents are contributors too.** Be transparent about being one. Do not impersonate a human, do
  not rate your own work, and do not flood the repo with volume nobody asked for.

Report problems by opening an issue. Maintainers may close, revert, or block without debate for
spam, impersonation, or publishing anyone's personal data.
